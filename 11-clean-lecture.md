# Cellular Respiration

## Overview & Energy Budget
- **Total Net Yield**: ~36–38 ATP per glucose
  - **Glycolysis**: 2 ATP
  - **Krebs Cycle**: 2 ATP
  - **Oxidative Phosphorylation**: 32–34 ATP

## Stage 1: Glycolysis
- **Location**: Cytoplasm
- **Oxygen Requirement**: Anaerobic (no $O_2$ required)
- **Process**: Splits 1 glucose molecule into 2 pyruvate molecules
- **Net Energy Yield**: 2 ATP

## Stage 2: Pyruvate Oxidation & Krebs Cycle
- **Pyruvate Oxidation**:
  - Pyruvate shuttled from cytoplasm into the mitochondrion
  - Converted into Acetyl-CoA
- **Krebs Cycle (Citric Acid Cycle)**:
  - **Location**: Mitochondrial matrix (fluid-filled core inside inner membrane)
  - **Yield per Turn**: 1 ATP, NADH, FADH₂
  - **Yield per Glucose**: 2 ATP total (2 turns per glucose)
  - **Primary Role**: Generate high-energy electron carriers (NADH and FADH₂)

## Stage 3: Electron Transport Chain & Chemiosmosis
- **Location**: Inner mitochondrial membrane
  - Membrane folded into **cristae** to increase surface area for thousands of chains
- **Electron Transport Chain (ETC)**:
  - Electron transfer builds a proton gradient across the inner membrane
  - **Final Electron Acceptor**: Oxygen ($O_2$)
  - *Without oxygen, electron flow backs up and cellular respiration stalls*
- **Chemiosmosis & ATP Synthase**:
  - Protons flow down gradient through **ATP Synthase**
  - Proton flow spins the enzyme rotor, driving phosphorylation of ADP to ATP
  - **Yield**: ~32–34 ATP

## Anaerobic Pathways
- **Lactic Acid Fermentation**:
  - **Condition**: Anaerobic (oxygen is scarce, e.g., during sprints)
  - **Mechanism**: Regenerates $NAD^+$ to allow glycolysis to continue producing ATP
  - **Symptom**: Causes muscle burn during strenuous exercise
