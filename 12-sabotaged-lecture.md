# BIO 201: Cellular Respiration (Lecture 7)

## 1. Glycolysis
- **Location:** Cytoplasm
- **Oxygen Requirement:** Anaerobic (No oxygen required)
- **Reaction:** 1 Glucose $\rightarrow$ 2 Pyruvate
- **Net Energy Yield:** 2 ATP

## 2. Pyruvate Oxidation
- **Process:** Pyruvate shuttled into mitochondrion
- **Conversion:** Pyruvate $\rightarrow$ Acetyl-CoA

## 3. Krebs Cycle (Citric Acid Cycle)
- **Location:** **Mitochondrial Matrix** (Fluid-filled core inside inner membrane)
  - *(Resolved per student: The Krebs cycle takes place in the mitochondrial matrix, resolving the initial transcript misstatement placing it in the cytoplasm)*
- **Outputs (per turn):**
  - NADH
  - $\text{FADH}_2$
  - 1 ATP (2 ATP total per glucose molecule)
- **Primary Function:** Produce high-energy electron carriers for the electron transport chain

## 4. Electron Transport Chain (ETC) & Chemiosmosis
- **Location:** Inner mitochondrial membrane
  - Membrane folds (**cristae**) maximize surface area to house thousands of transport chains
- **Mechanism:**
  - Electron flow creates a proton gradient across the membrane
  - **Final Electron Acceptor:** Oxygen ($\text{O}_2$)
  - *Without oxygen, the chain backs up and respiration stalls*
- **Chemiosmosis:**
  - Protons flow back down gradient through **ATP Synthase**
  - Rotor movement drives the phosphorylation of ADP $\rightarrow$ ATP
- **Yield:** ~32–34 ATP via oxidative phosphorylation

## 5. Total Energy Budget (per Glucose)
- **Glycolysis:** 2 ATP
- **Krebs Cycle:** 2 ATP
- **Oxidative Phosphorylation:** 32–34 ATP
- **Total Net Yield:** ~36–38 ATP

## 6. Anaerobic Pathways
- **Lactic Acid Fermentation:**
  - **Type:** Anaerobic
  - **Function:** Regenerates $\text{NAD}^+$ so glycolysis can continue when $\text{O}_2$ is scarce
  - **Physiological Impact:** Causes muscle burn after intense activity (e.g., sprints)
